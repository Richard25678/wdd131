Uganda

Uganda is a country in East Africa, located in the heart of the Great Lakes region. It is known for its welcoming communities, many languages, music, dance, and rich cultural traditions.

Its landscape includes Lake Victoria, the source of the Nile, fertile highlands, forests, savannas, and the Rwenzori Mountains. This variety of places and cultures makes Uganda a country I am proud to call home.
